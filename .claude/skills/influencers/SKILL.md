# Micro-influencer campaign

**Gate:** only for games at "double down" in `studio/strategy.md`
(D1 > 35%). If the game isn't there, say so and recommend the retention
fix instead. Paying to send players into a game that loses them is wasted money.

1. **Targeting**: TikTok/Shorts creators with 5k–100k followers in the
   game's niche (mobile games, cozy games, puzzle, "satisfying" content).
   What matters is average views over the last 10 posts, not followers;
   aim for views ≥ 20% of followers, and an audience in our top territories.
2. **Shortlist plan**: search terms and hashtags to find 20–30 candidates.
   Add them to `marketing/influencers.csv` (owner or Claude via web search).
3. **Offer ladder** (cheapest first): free + early access + credit in the
   game → custom in-game item named after them → small flat fee
   ($20–$100) for one dedicated 15–30 s video. Avoid per-install deals at
   this scale.
4. **Outreach**: short personalized DM/email per creator (reference a
   specific video of theirs, one sentence on the game, the offer, a
   no-pressure close). Draft them; **owner approves before sending**.
5. **Tracking**: one App Store Connect campaign link per creator
   (`?ct=<handle>&pt=<provider_token>`) so installs show up in App
   Analytics → Sources. Log in the CSV.
6. **Results** 7 days after each post: views, installs attributed, cost per
   install, D1 of those players if available. Re-book creators whose CPI
   beats our cutoff; record learnings in `studio/log.md`.
