# Social

Claude drafts; the owner posts (or a connected scheduler does) — always
confirm before anything is published.

## Channels
- **TikTok / Shorts / Reels** (main channel): 7–15 s vertical gameplay.
  The first second is the hook: a near-miss, a satisfying chain, a
  "can you beat this?", an impossible-looking level. On-screen text ≤ 8
  words. Native trending sound where possible. Same clip on all three.
- **X / Threads**: build-in-public — weekly numbers, what the AI manager
  recommended, what we did, what happened. Screenshots of real data.
- **Reddit**: r/iosgaming (follow its self-promo rules), r/AppHookup only
  for real deals, r/gamedev / r/IndieDev for dev stories, plus genre subs.
  Lead with value or the story, link last, reply to every comment.

## Output
1. 5–7 posts for the request, each: platform, hook (first 1 s), shot
   list of the clip (what happens second by second), caption, hashtags
   (3–5, niche > generic), CTA.
2. A 7-day calendar appended to `marketing/content-calendar.md`.
3. For a launch: a build-in-public post + a Reddit post draft.

## Learning loop
When views are logged in the calendar, compare hooks. Keep a running
"hooks that work" list at the top of `marketing/content-calendar.md` and
reuse winners across games.
